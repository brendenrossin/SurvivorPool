02 — What the colour change costs

Team colours collide. The row label is what saves it.

Measured, not guessed

I ran every team pair that co-occurs in a week through CIE76. 54 pairs sit under ΔE 20 — and four are byte-identical. If colour were carrying identity here, this chart would be unreadable.

Why it still works

Identity comes from the row label, not the fill — every row is one team, named in mono on the left, with the number in every cell. Colour is reinforcement, never the sole channel. That satisfies the accessibility rule the all-blue version satisfied a different way. The one thing it does cost: colour no longer encodes magnitude, which is exactly why you called for the number in every cell — without it the grid would carry no quantity at all.

Handled in the build

Label ink is computed per cell from the fill's relative luminance — PIT #FFB612 (0.55) and NO #D3BC8D (0.52) take dark text; LV #000000 and CHI #0B162A take white. That's the same class of bug as the tooltip: never eyeball contrast, compute it. Light fills also get a hairline border so they don't dissolve into the surface.

03 — Open question

Muted team colour, or greyscale?

You said "muted version, or like a greyscale heatmap" — they encode different things, so it's worth choosing deliberately. Toggle History style above to compare on real data.

  • Muted team — each past cell is that team's colour washed toward the background. History stays identifiable at a glance; you can trace a team's run across the row by hue. Magnitude comes only from the number.
  • Greyscale — past cells use a grey ramp keyed to that week's share, so the history region also encodes magnitude and the current week is the only colour on screen. Stronger emphasis, and the herd's density shows up as tone. Costs the hue-tracing.
My recommendation

Greyscale. The muted version spends colour on all 14 columns and weakens the very contrast that makes the current week pop — and since the number is in every cell anyway, hue-tracing is a small loss. Greyscale gives you one loud column and a quiet, still-quantitative history. But flip between them; this one is genuinely a taste call and you know the audience.

04 — Still to confirm

Three small ones

  • Number format — count or % of that week's survivors? Count is concrete ("14 people took DEN"); % is comparable across weeks as the pool shrinks 252 → 19. The tooltip shows both regardless.
  • Future weeks — the sheet already holds picks for weeks nobody has played yet. Right now the grid stops at the selected week. Show upcoming weeks too, or keep it to history?
  • Row cap — week 1 has 15 teams, so the grid is 15 rows there and 10 in quiet weeks. Any upper limit you want, or let it run?
